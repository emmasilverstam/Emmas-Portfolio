About me

I am a 28-year-old industrial design engineer who recently graduated from the Industrial Design program at Chalmers University of Technology with a master's degree in Interaction Design. My passion is simplifying complexity through technology and design. I am interested in graphic design, UI and UX, and aim to create seamless digital experiences by combining creativity with innovative technology.
